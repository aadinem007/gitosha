**Body:**

Most "100 SaaS ideas" lists are the same recycled 20 concepts with fake
revenue numbers attached. I got annoyed enough to build the opposite: a
weekly research pipeline that scores real ideas against actual competitor

The top two (84 and 81) were genuinely strong though, and I'm building both
